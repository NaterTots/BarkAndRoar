# Physical iPad check

Target supplied for this check: **iPad (A16), iPadOS 26.5, Safari**.
The parent confirmed basic taps and sound work on this device on 2026-09-25.
The initial bark/roar were revised, then accepted by the parent as “perfect.”
Steps 1–2 are accepted; **steps 3–7** below remain pending on the actual device.

1. On the iPad, open Safari and enter **https://natertots.github.io/BarkAndRoar/**.
   Set device volume low but audible. Wait until both faces and the large play
   triangle appear. Tap the triangle once.
2. Tap the lion, then the dog. Each very first animal tap should make its matching
   sound immediately. Confirm the roar is friendly and no louder or harsher than
   the bark. Listen to each alone and then both together. Report missing, delayed,
   distorted, unrecognizable or unpleasant sound.
3. Tap empty space near all four corners of each panel. Hold a face, drag it across
   the divider, and release. It should move only a little and spring back; the
   other animal must not take over that finger.
4. Put one finger on each animal at the same time, then add a third finger. Both
   animals should respond independently; extra fingers must not leave a face stuck.
5. Turn between landscape and portrait, including while holding a face. Portrait
   should show lion above dog, with large faces and no scrolling or clipping.
   Show/hide Safari's address toolbar and confirm the canvas still fits.
6. While a sound is playing, switch to another app. Sound should stop. Return,
   tap the play triangle, then each animal. Repeat after locking and unlocking
   the screen. There should be no stuck face or burst of old sounds.
7. Use repeated taps, holds, drags and simultaneous touches for **15–30 minutes**.
   You can do this yourself. Watch for silence, distortion, freezing, or Safari
   reloading with “a problem occurred.”

Reply with: **test date, iPadOS version, minutes tested, whether both first taps
made sound, whether the clips sound friendly/balanced, and any failed step**.
The exact live build/commit is also available at
**https://natertots.github.io/BarkAndRoar/build-info.json**; copy its `commit` value
into your reply if the build has changed since the checklist was sent.
If a failure occurs, describe the
smallest sequence that reproduces it and whether reloading fixes it. No account,
token, installation, or GitHub settings change is needed.
